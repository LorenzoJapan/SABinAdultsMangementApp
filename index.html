Definitions

Key terms used in the 2026 statement, in plain words.

About

What this app is based on, and what it is not.

What the app covers

  • Adults only. Children have separate guidance in the statement.
  • Risk stratification: low-risk vs increased-risk SAB (Statement 1, Figure 1).
  • Follow-up blood cultures (Statement 2), TTE (3), TEE (4), whole-body imaging (5).
  • Treatment duration (Statements 6 and 7) and the start-date rule.

What it does not cover

  • Which antibiotic to use (MRSA vs MSSA) — to be addressed in future IDSA/ESCMID publications.
  • Exact duration for each specific focus (e.g., endocarditis, osteomyelitis).
  • Pediatric or neonatal patients.

Clinical educational tool

For use by clinicians (physicians, advanced practice providers, pharmacists) as an educational aid that summarizes the 2026 IDSA/ESCMID consensus statements. It does not replace clinical judgment or infectious diseases consultation, which the panel strongly encourages. Risk is dynamic — reassess as the patient's course evolves. The listed risk factors are not exhaustive.

The guideline content is the copyrighted property of IDSA. Use of the guideline within software requires permission from IDSA.

Privacy

  • No protected health information (PHI). The app never asks for names, record numbers or other patient identifiers — please don't type any in.
  • Answers stay on this device, are not saved, and clear when you tap Start over or close the app.
  • Nothing is sent over the internet — no accounts, tracking or analytics.
  • Designed for HIPAA-conscious use.
